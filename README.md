# Civicfy — Crowdsourced Civic Issue Reporting and Resolution System

**Civicfy** is a frontend prototype developed as a **hackathon project** to create a simple and transparent platform for reporting and tracking everyday civic issues. The project was developed by **Team Ecolytes** and focuses on using technology to make communication between citizens and civic authorities more efficient.

---

## 🏆 Hackathon

**24 Hours of ReCode Hackathon — NMIMS College**

Civicfy was developed as part of the **24-hour ReCode Hackathon at NMIMS**, where our team worked on designing and presenting the solution within a limited time frame.

---

## 🎯 Problem We Addressed

Citizens regularly face civic problems such as **potholes, broken streetlights, overflowing garbage bins, and other local infrastructure issues**. However, the lack of a simple reporting and tracking system can make it difficult for authorities to identify, prioritize, and resolve these problems efficiently.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

We designed **Civicfy**, a **simple and mobile-friendly interface** that allows citizens to report civic issues and track their progress.

The prototype demonstrates how a centralized platform could make civic issue reporting **easier, faster, and more transparent** for both citizens and authorities.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 📸 Report civic issues using **photos, location, voice, or text**
* 🗺️ View reported issues through an **interactive map**
* ⚡ Prioritize issues based on **urgency and number of reports**
* 🏢 Admin dashboard for **municipal authorities**
* 🔄 Route complaints to the **appropriate department**
* 🔔 Track complaint status and receive **updates**
* 📊 View **analytics and reporting trends**

---

## 🛠️ Current Scope

For the hackathon, Civicfy was developed primarily as a **frontend prototype** to demonstrate the concept, interface, and user flow.

The **backend, database, real-time map integration, automated department routing, and live notification system** are part of the future development roadmap.
