# Orbit — Student Productivity & Academic Management Platform

## 🏆 Hackathon

**24 Hours of ReCode Hackathon — NMIMS College**

**Orbit** was developed by our team during the **24-hour ReCode Hackathon at NMIMS College**. The project focuses on solving a common problem faced by students — managing academics, deadlines, resources, feedback, productivity, and well-being across multiple disconnected platforms.

---

## Overview

**Orbit** is a futuristic and customizable **student productivity and academic management platform** designed to bring different aspects of academic life into one unified system.

Instead of using separate platforms for tasks, resources, academic tracking, feedback, and productivity, Orbit brings everything together through a single intelligent dashboard.

Orbit works as a **Command Center for Academic Life**, helping students understand **what to prioritize, when to focus, and how to improve**.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Students and professors often rely on multiple platforms for:

* Learning and academic resources
* Assignment and task management
* Notes and communication
* Academic performance tracking
* Professor feedback
* Personal productivity

Because these systems are disconnected, students often struggle with:

* Knowing what to prioritize
* Missing important deadlines
* Tracking feedback and improvements
* Finding resources quickly
* Balancing academic performance and productivity

There is a need for a system that brings these elements together in one place.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

We designed **Orbit** as a unified academic operating system that connects productivity, learning, academic performance, feedback, and well-being.

The platform is designed around three main modes:

### 👨‍🎓 Student Mode

Personal productivity, tasks, learning, resources, and academic progress.

### 📚 Class Mode

Subjects, lectures, academic performance, and learning resources.

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher Mode

Teaching, student monitoring, feedback, and academic guidance.

These modes are connected to create a smoother workflow between students and professors.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Priority Hub — Task Gravity System

Orbit introduces a **Task Gravity System** that helps students prioritize tasks based on:

* **Urgency** — How close the deadline is
* **Academic Weight** — How much the task contributes to marks

This helps students focus on tasks that have the **highest academic impact** instead of simply completing tasks in random order.

---

### 2. Contextual UI Adaptation

The interface adapts according to the student's current activity.

For example, during a live lecture, Orbit can switch to **Focus Mode**, showing only relevant lecture content, notes, and resources while minimizing distractions.

---

### 3. GitHub-Style Feedback System

Professor feedback is organized like issues in a development workflow.

Students can:

* View feedback
* Respond to comments
* Work on improvements
* Mark feedback as resolved
* Track their academic progress

This makes feedback more **actionable and trackable**.

---

### 4. Resource Shelf

Orbit provides a centralized academic resource library where students can:

* Search resources across subjects
* Filter resources by subject, type, and date
* Access PDFs and videos
* Preview resources without leaving the platform

---

### 5. Knowledge Graph

The **Knowledge Graph** visually connects subjects and topics.

This helps students understand how different concepts are related and encourages **concept-based learning** rather than studying topics in isolation.

---

### 6. Mental Health & Productivity Tracking

Orbit also considers student well-being through features such as:

* Monthly mood tracking
* Journaling
* Focus tracking
* **Earn Focus** productivity system

This allows students to monitor both their **academic productivity and personal well-being**.

---

### 7. Achievement Guidance System

Instead of simply displaying achievements, Orbit provides **actionable guidance**.

For example:

> “Attend 3 more lectures to reach 75% attendance and unlock this achievement.”

This turns academic data into **clear and achievable actions**.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Orbit follows a **high-density dashboard approach**, allowing students to access multiple types of information without making the interface feel overwhelming.

### Design Principles

* Minimal and professional interface
* High information density with clear hierarchy
* Glassmorphism-inspired panels
* Blue, black, white, and grey visual system
* Typography based on information priority
* Draggable and resizable panels
* Command-based navigation
* Modular dashboard design

---

## 🛠️ Technology & Concepts

The project demonstrates concepts such as:

* Dashboard-based Information Architecture
* Task Priority Algorithms
* Knowledge Graph Visualization
* Context-Aware UI
* Modular UI Panels
* Single Page Application (SPA) Navigation
* Interactive Resource Preview System

---

## 🌟 Impact

Orbit is more than a traditional productivity tool.

It acts as a **decision-making system for students** and a **guidance system for professors**.

By bringing scattered academic information into one place, Orbit aims to reduce academic chaos and turn information into **structured, meaningful, and actionable insights**.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion

Orbit transforms a **fragmented academic experience into a unified and intelligent system**.

It doesn't just show students their data — **it helps them understand what to focus on next.**
